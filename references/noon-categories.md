# Known Kayan Store category mappings

Use these as mapping candidates, not substitutes for the current Noon category catalog. Select the narrowest available leaf category and copy its current Noon category ID from the supplied catalog/template.

| Product group | Preferred Noon leaf category |
|---|---|
| General keychains | Fashion > Accessories > Keychains |
| Men's keychains | Fashion > Men's Accessories > Keychains |
| Car keychains | Automotive > Interior Accessories > Keychains |
| Car compasses | Automotive > Interior Accessories > Compasses |
| Model aircraft and aircraft kits | Toys & Games > Hobbies > Models & Model Kits > Aircraft Models & Kits |
| Die-cast vehicles | Toys & Games > Hobbies > Models & Model Kits > Die-cast Models |
| Display shelves and stands | Home & Kitchen > Home Decor > Decor Accessories > Display Stands / Shelves |
| Wall decor | Home & Kitchen > Home Decor > Wall Decor |
| Desk lamps | Home & Kitchen > Lighting > Table & Desk Lamps |
| Special/decorative lamps | Home & Kitchen > Lighting > Novelty / Decorative Lighting |

## Category rules

- Exclude the source category `action-figure` when the user requests the Kayan Store catalog workflow described by this skill.
- Exclude mobile-accessory products already uploaded to the marketplace when building a new-product retry file.
- Do not invent category IDs. Read them from the current Noon catalog supplied for the target marketplace and template version.
- When a product could fit several leaves, choose the leaf matching its primary function, not its decorative theme.
